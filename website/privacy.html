The short version: MAC Addy Spoofer runs entirely on your local machine. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to external servers — ever. Your network configuration, MAC addresses, and activity stay on your device.

Data Collection

MAC Addy Spoofer operates entirely on your local machine. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All network information, MAC addresses, history, and settings remain on your device.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Your MAC addresses or network interface names
  • Your location, IP address, or network topology
  • Usage patterns, session duration, or feature usage
  • Crash reports (unless you send them manually to our support email)
  • Any personally identifiable information of any kind

Local Storage

The application stores your preferences and history locally on your device using Electron's localStorage and macOS standard storage. This includes:

  • Interface mapping configuration (which macOS interface IDs map to Wi-Fi, Ethernet, Bluetooth)
  • Vendor preferences and MAC address history
  • Automation timer settings
  • App behavior preferences (confirmations, notifications, etc.)
  • In-app purchase status and App Store transaction identifiers

This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by resetting preferences within the app (Settings → Reset to Defaults) or by uninstalling MAC Addy Spoofer.

In-App Purchases

MAC Addy Spoofer offers optional in-app purchases processed entirely through Apple's Mac App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. All payment processing, receipt validation, and purchase management is handled exclusively by Apple.

We only store a local record of your purchase status (active/inactive) to determine which features are unlocked. This data does not leave your device.

For subscription management, cancellation, or refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com or manage via System Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Network Communications

MAC Addy Spoofer does not make any network connections of its own. The app:

  • Does not "phone home" — there are no background network requests to SouriApps servers.
  • Does not send analytics — no telemetry, usage data, or crash reports are transmitted automatically.
  • Does not connect to any external service — all operations are performed locally using macOS system calls (ifconfig).

The only external interaction is with Apple's App Store infrastructure for purchase verification, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

No Analytics or Tracking

MAC Addy Spoofer does not include any analytics SDKs, telemetry agents, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or third-party monitoring tools. There is no Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Sentry, Crashlytics, or equivalent library in the application.

Your use of MAC Addy Spoofer is entirely private. We have no visibility into which features you use, how often you use the app, or anything about your network activity.

Third-Party Services

MAC Addy Spoofer does not integrate with any third-party data collection, advertising, or analytics services. The only external interaction is with Apple's App Store infrastructure for purchase verification, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Security

MAC Addy Spoofer is designed with security as a priority:

  • All application data is stored locally in macOS app-sandboxed storage
  • No user data is transmitted to third-party servers under any circumstances
  • The application uses Hardened Runtime, making it resistant to code injection
  • MAC address changes use macOS's native ifconfig utility via privilege escalation — no custom kernel extensions or drivers
  • The app is distributed through the Mac App Store or as a notarized binary, verified by Apple

Children's Privacy

MAC Addy Spoofer is a professional network utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. In practice, this is moot — we collect no information from anyone.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your information at all times. You can:

  • View all stored settings in the Settings view of the app
  • Delete all application data by uninstalling MAC Addy Spoofer
  • Reset preferences at any time with Settings → Reset to Defaults
  • Export your history to CSV via File → Export History as CSV
  • Manage your App Store purchase history through your Apple ID

Because we hold no personal data about you on our servers, there is nothing for us to export, transfer, or delete on request — you already have full control.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application or applicable law. The current version is always available at macaddy.souriapps.net/privacy and within the app under About → Privacy Policy.
