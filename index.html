MAC Addy Spoofer is a privacy utility that randomizes and manages the hardware addresses of your network interfaces. Vendor-specific generation, scheduled automation, and a full change history — all on-device with zero telemetry.

Last updated: February 2026

MAC Addy Spoofer is designed to operate on-device and collect as little data as possible.

Data the app processes

  • Configuration settings you choose (timers, vendor preferences, interface selection).
  • Change history generated by your actions.

Data storage

All data is stored locally on your device. The app does not transmit anything to any server.

Why does Apply MAC fail?

MAC changes require root/sudo privileges. The app calls mac-changer-macos.sh. Run it once manually with sudo from Terminal to confirm it works.

Why doesn't the app detect my real MAC?

The app reads MACs via ifconfig through Electron IPC. Ensure interface names in Settings match your Mac's actual IDs (usually en0 for Wi-Fi).

Will MAC changes persist after reboot?

On macOS, changes made via ifconfig are temporary and revert on reboot. This is by design.

How do I set up sudo access?

Add a sudoers entry: username ALL=(root) NOPASSWD: /path/to/scripts/mac-changer-macos.sh

Clone MAC Address
Enter the MAC address you want to clone onto this interface. This will populate the MAC field — click Apply MAC to apply it to the OS.
